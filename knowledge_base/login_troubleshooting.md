# Login Problems

## Symptoms
- User cannot sign in.
- Login fails despite entering credentials.
- Account may be locked.

## Troubleshooting
1. Confirm username and Caps Lock status.
2. Do not share the password with support staff through chat.
3. Check the official password-reset process.
4. If the account is locked, contact the IT helpdesk for verification and unlock procedures.
5. Confirm whether the issue happens on another approved device.

## Escalation
Account lockouts and identity verification should be handled through official IT procedures.
