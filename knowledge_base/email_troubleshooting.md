# Email Problems

## Symptoms
- Cannot send email.
- Cannot receive recent messages.
- Mail client repeatedly asks for a password.

## Troubleshooting
1. Verify internet connectivity.
2. Check whether the mailbox is full.
3. Confirm the email address is correct.
4. Check the service status if the organisation provides one.
5. Restart the mail application.
6. If the client repeatedly requests credentials, do not store or send the password in chat; use the official account recovery process.

## Escalation
Escalate when mailbox or server-side settings may be involved.
