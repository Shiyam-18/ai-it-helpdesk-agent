# VPN Connection Problems

## Symptoms
- VPN client cannot connect.
- VPN connects and immediately disconnects.
- Internal company resources are unreachable.

## Troubleshooting
1. Confirm normal internet access before starting the VPN.
2. Verify the VPN server or profile is correct.
3. Re-enter credentials carefully.
4. Restart the VPN application.
5. Restart the computer.
6. Check whether the account is locked or expired with the IT team.
7. If available, try another network to distinguish local network issues from VPN issues.

## Escalation
Do not repeatedly guess VPN settings. Escalate if the account or server may require administrative changes.
